Latent Machinery is a software consultancy. We design, build, and maintain AI-assisted systems that take recurring work off founder-led businesses: intake, reporting, follow-up, and the operational memory that ties them together.

Services

Build

Custom systems scoped to one job that matters: a workflow, a data pipeline, an assistant that does real work. Fixed-scope projects with a defined deliverable.

Operate

Monthly retainers for teams that need someone to own the systems after launch: monitoring, changes, and the judgment calls software can't make on its own.

Advise

Fractional technical leadership for founders deciding what to build, what to buy, and what to leave alone.

How we work

Engagements are remote and run under a written services agreement. Projects are quoted as a fixed scope; retainers bill monthly. Invoices are paid by bank transfer. Clients keep ownership of everything built for them and of the data it runs on.
